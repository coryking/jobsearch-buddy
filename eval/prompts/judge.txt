You are evaluating how well a job description was stripped for vector search.

WHAT YOU'RE LOOKING AT: One original job description and its stripped version.
You will judge many such pairs across diverse companies and roles — defense
contractors, AI startups, fintech, enterprise SaaS, and more. Apply the same
rubric consistently regardless of industry or role type.

WHY WE STRIP: The stripped text becomes a single vector in a search index
containing thousands of job postings from 100+ different companies. Users
search across all companies with natural language queries like "robotics
engineer with security clearance near DC" or "PM roles at companies working
on internet privacy." Every word in the stripped text influences the vector.

Text that appears identically across thousands of postings — EEO statements,
generic benefits, legal disclaimers — pushes every job's vector toward the same
point, drowning out the signal that makes each job unique. That shared text
must be removed. But content specific to THIS company or THIS role is the
signal that helps the right job surface for the right query — it must stay.

THE OPERATION IS DELETION ONLY: The strip model removes lines from the
original. It should never rewrite, paraphrase, summarize, or add text.
If a line appears in the stripped version, it should be character-for-character
identical to the original.

WHAT SHOULD BE REMOVED (interchangeable across postings):
- EEO / equal opportunity statements
- Generic benefits lists (the standard health/dental/vision/401k/PTO package)
- Drug-free workplace policies
- Accessibility / accommodation request instructions
- Legal disclaimers, privacy policy links, applicant data notices
- Recruiter scam warnings
- "We encourage all candidates to apply" diversity encouragement paragraphs

WHAT SHOULD BE KEPT (differentiates this posting in cross-company search):
- Role responsibilities, qualifications, tech stack, team/project descriptions
- Company description, mission, products, named projects — anything specific
  to THIS company that wouldn't appear on other companies' postings. Example:
  Cloudflare's "Project Galileo" or Anthropic's research directions are
  company-differentiating, not boilerplate, even though they aren't role requirements
- Compensation details: salary ranges, equity, RSUs, signing bonuses
- Remote / hybrid / onsite details, location, reporting structure
- Eligibility requirements: export control, citizenship, security clearance,
  background checks, work authorization — these are hard gates that determine
  who can apply, not legal boilerplate. A user searching "jobs that don't
  require clearance" or "roles open to non-US-citizens" needs this info
  in the vector
- Benefits that are specific or unusual (100% coverage, fertility benefits,
  specific dollar amounts) — only the generic "standard package" list should go

THE KEY TEST: Would you find nearly identical text on thousands of other
companies' postings? If yes, remove it. If it's specific to this company
or role, keep it.

CRITICAL FAILURES (automatic score cap of 2):
- Hallucination: adding text not in the original
- Rephrasing: changing wording instead of only deleting lines

Score the stripped version 1-5:

  5: All boilerplate removed, all differentiating content preserved, no rephrasing
  4: Nearly perfect — minor boilerplate remnants OR trivial omissions
  3: Decent but noticeable issues — some boilerplate left OR meaningful content lost
  2: Significant problems — important content removed, lots of boilerplate remaining,
     OR any rephrasing/hallucination
  1: Failed — barely stripped (output ≈ input), critical content lost, or substantial
     hallucination

Respond with ONLY valid JSON (no markdown fences):
{
  "score": <1-5>,
  "reasoning": "<1-2 sentences explaining your score>"
}
