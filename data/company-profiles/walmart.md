# Walmart

Walmart Inc. is the world's largest company by revenue and the largest private employer in the world. Headquartered in Bentonville, Arkansas, Walmart reported $713B in revenue for the twelve months ending January 31, 2026 (fiscal year 2026), a 4.7% year-over-year increase. The company employs approximately 2.1 million associates worldwide, operates more than 10,750 stores in 19 countries, and serves roughly 270 million customers and members per week through stores, e-commerce, and mobile. Walmart trades on the Nasdaq (WMT), having switched from the NYSE in December 2025. Sam Walton founded the company in 1962 in Rogers, Arkansas; it remains controlled by the Walton family.

John Furner became President and CEO on February 1, 2026, succeeding Doug McMillon, who had led the company since 2014. Furner started at Walmart as an hourly associate in 1993 and rose through merchandising, operations, and sourcing before running Walmart U.S. since 2019. Suresh Kumar serves as EVP, Global Chief Technology Officer, and Chief Development Officer, bringing prior senior leadership experience from Google, Microsoft, and Amazon.

## Business Segments and Strategy

Walmart operates three reporting segments: Walmart U.S. (nearly 4,600 stores), Sam's Club (warehouse membership clubs with operations in the U.S., Mexico, and China), and Walmart International. The core business is mass-market retail across grocery, general merchandise, health and wellness, and pharmacy. Walmart competes directly with Amazon, Costco, Target, and Kroger. Walmart holds approximately 8.6% of the overall U.S. retail market; Amazon, Walmart, and Costco together captured about 46% of all incremental U.S. retail sales growth in recent quarters.

Several higher-margin businesses have become increasingly central to Walmart's financial profile. Walmart Connect, the advertising and retail media business, generated $6.4B in revenue in fiscal 2025, growing 46% year-over-year. Walmart's CFO has noted that advertising accounts for nearly one-third of the company's operating income. Walmart Marketplace, the third-party seller platform, makes Walmart.com the second-largest U.S. e-commerce marketplace by volume, reaching 160 million omnichannel shoppers per week. The U.S. e-commerce business turned profitable in Q1 of fiscal 2026, with e-commerce sales rising 22%.

## Technology Organization: Walmart Global Tech

Walmart Global Tech is the company's technology arm, employing 15,000+ software engineers, data scientists, product managers, cybersecurity experts, and related roles. The organization maintains offices in Bentonville (co-located with the new 350-acre corporate campus), Sunnyvale (where Walmart signed Silicon Valley's largest lease in years -- 338,000 sq ft at Tech Corners, with the 719,000 sq ft Moffett Green campus accommodating up to 3,600 tech employees), Seattle, Dallas, Hoboken, and Atlanta.

The tech stack operates at enormous scale: 100,000+ routers and switches, 450,000 wireless access points, 5 million connected devices, and 25,000+ engineers developing solutions across 10,500+ stores and thousands of fulfillment and distribution centers. Core platform technologies include SONiC (open-source network OS deployed in data centers), L3AF (eBPF-based network function orchestration), OpenStack (170,000+ computing cores), and the Element ML/AI platform for model development and deployment. The engineering blog on Medium (Walmart Global Tech Blog) publishes regularly on topics including demand forecasting (Spark, Hive), network infrastructure, and ML platform architecture. Walmart has contributed 60+ open-source projects and maintains an internal "innersource" culture using GitHub.

## AI and Automation Investments

Walmart has made AI a central strategic pillar. The company developed proprietary LLMs called Wallaby, trained on decades of internal data, and partnered with OpenAI in October 2025 to enable shopping through ChatGPT. Four internal "super agents" have been deployed: Marty (for sellers/suppliers), Sparky (for shoppers), an Associate Agent, and a Developer Agent. AI-powered tools deployed to the 1.5M U.S. associate base include real-time translation and task management (reducing shift planning from 90 to 30 minutes). The generative AI search engine has been credited by executives as a driver of e-commerce growth.

On the supply chain side, Walmart invested $520M in Symbotic's AI robotics platform, deploying automation across 42 regional distribution centers. More than 60% of U.S. stores receive freight from automated distribution centers, and over half of e-commerce fulfillment volume moves through automated systems. U.S. net delivery costs per order dropped 40% in the most recent fiscal quarter.

## Organizational Culture and Work Environment

Glassdoor lists 4,517 reviews for Walmart Global Tech specifically, and over 141,000 reviews for Walmart overall. The overall Walmart rating is 3.4/5; 56% of employees would recommend the company, and 51% have a positive business outlook. On Blind, Walmart has a 3.3/5 rating across 3,161 reviews, with work-life balance rated highest at 3.5/5 and management rated lowest at 2.7/5.

**Decision-making and bureaucracy.** Reviews on both Glassdoor and Blind describe vertical hierarchies and horizontal functional teams that create silos and miscommunication. Multiple reviews mention layers of approvals that slow development. The company's own stated rationale for 2024-2025 layoffs was to "speed up decision-making" and "remove layers and complexity," which implicitly acknowledges the structural challenge. One Glassdoor review describes the environment as a place where "mediocrity prevails."

**Pace and intensity.** The experience varies significantly by team and location. Some reviews describe good work-life balance and steady pace; others describe being contacted on weekends and working 12-hour days. A recurring theme in negative reviews involves time zone coordination with India-based teams requiring early morning or late evening availability. Peak business periods (holidays, major launches) intensify workloads.

**Management.** Management quality is the most polarizing topic across review platforms. Positive reviews describe supportive managers and opportunities for growth. Negative reviews cite inconsistent management, political dynamics, and performance evaluation processes that feel arbitrary -- one Blind reviewer wrote that managers "will write anything in your feedback and decide if you should be promoted or put on PIP." The 2.7/5 management rating on Blind is notably lower than other dimensions.

**Technical culture.** Reviews describe the tech stack positively, with several noting continuous improvement and modernization. The scale of the systems (processing for 270M weekly customers) provides genuine technical challenge. Walmart's open-source engagement -- SONiC, L3AF, Electrode, OneOps, 60+ projects -- reflects investment in community participation, with annual awards for open-source contributions and quarterly reviews of open-source goals.

**Remote work policy.** In 2024, Walmart required corporate employees in satellite offices (Dallas, Atlanta, Toronto) to relocate to Bentonville, Hoboken, or Southern California, with a deadline between August 2024 and January 2025. Employees unable to relocate were asked to leave. The company operates a hybrid schedule requiring in-office presence for the majority of the work week. This was controversial internally; employees pushed back in a companywide Zoom call, and some chose to quit. The new Bentonville campus opened in January 2025.

**Compensation.** Software engineer total compensation ranges from approximately $116K at Level 1 to $323K at Level 6, with principal engineers in the $276K-$440K range (per Levels.fyi data from February 2026). Stock compensation and bonuses increase meaningfully at senior levels. Benefits include health/dental/vision, 401(k) matched up to 6%, stock purchase matched up to 15%, Walmart+ membership, and education benefits. However, a 2024 Blind post flagged benefits cuts for Global Tech employees specifically.

**Layoffs and restructuring.** Walmart cut approximately 1,500 corporate technology roles in May 2025, affecting engineers, data scientists, software developers, and IT project managers. An additional 381 jobs were cut from the Sunnyvale office in June. The stated focus shifted toward "high-impact initiatives" including real-time inventory systems and AI-driven demand forecasting, with positions tied to legacy systems and non-critical automation being phased out. A further round of hundreds of corporate cuts occurred in July 2025.

## Competitive Context

Walmart's primary competitors are Amazon (in e-commerce and advertising), Costco and Sam's Club (warehouse clubs), Target (mass-market retail), and Kroger (grocery). The company's strategic trajectory is moving from pure retail toward a platform model where advertising, marketplace, and membership revenues subsidize lower-margin retail operations -- a shift that mirrors Amazon's evolution. The technology organization is central to this transition, which positions tech roles as increasingly important to the company's margin strategy rather than purely cost-center functions.
